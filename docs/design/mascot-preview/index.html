Meet the guard of your data

Three art styles for the Secure Store padlock mascot. All three use the same mood classes, so whichever you pick, the pages only ever switch one class on the SVG. Pick a style, a name, and we build the templates.

Jump to a mood

A Mochi

Soft rounded body, flat pastel fill, big glossy eyes, blush. No outlines. The cuddliest of the three.

B Sticker

Thick ink outline with a white sticker border and dot eyes, like a chat sticker. Reads best at small sizes such as the nav icon.

C Keyhole guard

Shield-shaped lock with a guard cap, a keyhole for a nose, and a key in hand. The most "security" flavoured.

How it plugs into the app (after you pick)

  • One template partial renders the chosen SVG with class="mascot mascot--<mood>".
  • Pages pick the starting mood: login/register suspicious, files smiling, empty list sleepy, errors confused.
  • A small static script swaps the class for focus, upload/download (htmx events) and form results.
  • Under reduced motion, nothing moves, but every mood keeps its face.
Preview only. Not embedded in the app binary. One-shot moods (angry, unlocked) replay every few seconds in the galleries.
