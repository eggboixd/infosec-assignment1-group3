Pick a palette

Four replacements for the cream theme, each on a mini copy of the real UI and with Gembi recoloured to match. Every text colour is checked against the background it sits on and is at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA). Open "Lowest text contrast" under each palette for the full list.

Chosen: 3, Strawberry milk. The app also darkened its input borders to at least 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11), so the real form fields have stronger outlines than the mock ones here. See entry 13 in docs/decisions-frontend.md.
