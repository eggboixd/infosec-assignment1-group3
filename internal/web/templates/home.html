How to apply

  1. Fill in your applicant dataName, NIK, date of birth, address and contact details.
  2. Upload six documentsKTP, CV, transcript, recommendation letter, parents' salary slip and a short video.
  3. SubmitChoose a scholarship and press Submit. One set of documents works for both.

How your data is protected

Every field, every document and even which scholarship you applied to is stored encrypted three times, with AES-256, DES and RC4, in CTR mode (never ECB) and with an HMAC that catches tampering. Only you can read it back after logging in.

The benchmarks page compares how fast the three ciphers are.
